Hero skull anatomy

Adapted from the Visible Human Male cranium/maxilla and mandible in:
Seyed Hamidreza Alavi and Malte Asseln (2026), Visible Human Full-Skeleton 3D Bone Models (BoneHub).
https://doi.org/10.57967/hf/10464
https://huggingface.co/datasets/BoneHub/visible-human-3d-models

The dataset derives from the aligned Visible Human CT release by:
T. E. Andreassen, D. R. Hume, L. D. Hamilton, K. E. Walker, S. E. Higinbotham,
and K. B. Shelburne (2023), Three Dimensional Lower Extremity Musculoskeletal
Geometry of the Visible Human Female and Male. Scientific Data 10, 34.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01905-2

Changes: reduced the meshes for the web, oriented and scaled them, posed the
mandible open, and baked surface fitting and ambient occlusion data.
The dental surfaces were reshaped locally to seat separately modeled crowns.
A contoured upper socket ridge and the upper crowns were resurfaced with the
cranium as one mesh. Enamel colors blend into the bone along the socket ridge.
